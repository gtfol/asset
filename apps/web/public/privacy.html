Your financial data

Updated October 17, 2026

Asset is operated by gtfol, LLC. This notice describes the financial information used by Asset alongside the gtfol privacy policy.

What you choose to connect

Asset processes the account names, balances, transactions, investment records, public wallet addresses, and imported files you provide or authorize through a connection. Google provides your sign-in identity and verified email. Venmo receipt forwarding processes payment details from the email address you configure. Forward only the receipts you want Asset to process.

Apple financial data

With your permission, Asset reads the accounts, balances, and transactions you select in Apple’s authorization screen. Before uploading them, Asset asks separately for permission to store them in your private workspace and make them available in the web app and on your other devices. This includes account names, merchants, dates, and amounts. Apple account syncing requires an authenticator code on new sign-ins. No Apple account access is available until Apple approves Asset’s FinanceKit entitlement.

Storage and use

Your financial data is used to provide your account history, budgets, spending analysis, alerts, and net worth. It is not sold or used for advertising. Hosted workspaces use Vercel and Supabase; forwarded receipts use Resend. These providers process information to operate Asset. Connections use encrypted transport, access-controlled storage, and encrypted credentials. Native cached data is protected by iOS device encryption and the app’s unlock screen.

Your controls

You can revoke Apple access in iPhone Settings to stop new reads. In Asset’s Apple accounts screen, “Disconnect and remove Apple data” removes the synced Apple records from your workspace and preserves records independently imported from CSV. Previously downloaded backups remain under your control. You can pause receipt forwarding, disconnect linked institutions, and export your workspace. Contact team@gtfol.dev to request deletion or help with data access. We retain workspace data while you use the service and delete it when requested, subject to legal obligations and provider backup retention.
