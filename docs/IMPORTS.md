# Importing Copilot transactions

Choose **Import** in the web app, or **Settings → Import CSV** on iPhone. Asset recognizes Copilot's account/mask/status/category columns and offers a combined preview. Generic single-account CSV import remains available for other exports.

Review each account mapping and account type. Match an existing account when its history is already connected. Otherwise Asset creates an account with an unknown balance. Account names plus masks distinguish multiple cards with the same name. Currency is chosen explicitly because Copilot's export has no currency column.

The importer preserves dates, descriptions, exact cents, categories and parent groups, notes, spending exclusions, pending status, tags, and recurring labels. Copilot Regular amounts become spending when positive and refunds when negative. Income remains income and is included in cash flow; Copilot's budget-exclusion flag does not hide income from Asset totals. Internal Transfer remains a transfer excluded from income/spending totals. Source review status is not exported. By default, posted records with a category, income/transfer type, or exclusion are treated as reviewed; pending and uncategorized purchases remain unreviewed. The web import offers an opt-out.

For older imports, uploading the same CSV offers **Update imported history** after preview. This separately corrects review status and income exclusions for exact previously imported identities, including history now linked to banks. It leaves unrelated transactions and existing categories, notes, transfers, and excluded purchases unchanged. Ordinary re-imports do not reset review choices.

Imports are atomic and additive. Invalid rows block the entire Copilot import. Identical purchases within a file remain separate records. A full import identity plus occurrence number prevents replaying the same export, even after user display edits. Pending records with the same identity can settle on a later export. Re-import does not overwrite category/notes edits or resurrect deleted rows. Existing non-Copilot transactions are matched one-to-one by account, date, amount, type, currency, and normalized name. Records whose bank descriptions, posted dates, amounts, or transfer classifications differ need review because the export does not include provider IDs.

Balances, investments, budgets, and complete account coverage are not inferred from the transaction export. Pending records remain excluded from spending totals until settled. Historical imports do not generate transaction notifications.

After connecting a bank, choose **Connections → Link imported history**. Matching account masks are suggested for review. Linking keeps the imported account identity and name, adopts the bank's live balance, and combines matching transactions one-to-one while preserving imported categories, notes, exclusions, recurring labels, tags, and edits. Dates can match the bank's posted or authorized date. Ambiguous descriptions remain separate for review. Older CSV history stays available without claiming the bank supplied complete history for those years. Re-importing the same Copilot export remains idempotent.

For accounts already linked, **Settings & data → Match imported history** previews unmatched bank records alongside CSV history. A match requires the same account, currency, amount and compatible direction, a supporting merchant/ACH description, and dates no more than seven days apart. Both records must have only one possible counterpart. Pending, ambiguous, and manually edited bank records stay separate. Combining preserves the CSV identity and user decisions while attaching future bank updates to that row. The same date tolerance is used during new syncs so posting delays do not routinely create extra copies.

Files are limited to 10 MB and 10,000 rows. Hosted responses are compressed and limited to 4 MB on the wire, with a 32 MB workspace limit before compression. Parsing stores normalized fields without a redundant copy of every CSV cell. Full backups preserve import identity and metadata. The source file is not written to the repository or transmitted to analytics or AI services by the app.
